Who we are

A matchmaker
with no eyes.

We're a small group of friends who have had a good share of experience on dating apps, and we believe our deepest connections were made based on our own efforts.

We pair you based on a handful of thoughtful questions and a gut feeling, then coordinate a blind date in a safe public place. Before setting you up, we give the guys date ideas and advice on how to treat the girls on dates :)

Our mission

Slow down.
Take your time.

Modern dating can feel more competitive and more fast paced than the Wall Street Stock Exchange, but it doesnt need to be. We're aiming to encourage people to connect before they judge. Dating is not a chore.

Radical Anonymity

No photos. No names. No social links. No AI slop

Meaningful Matching

Safe by Design
